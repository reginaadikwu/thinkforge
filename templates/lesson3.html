Lesson 3

Giving Clear Instructions

Welcome back! 👋

In the last lesson, you learned how to break a big problem into smaller steps.

But those steps also need to be clear.

Imagine you tell someone: "Make a sandwich."

That's a goal, but it doesn't tell them exactly what to do.

A clearer set of instructions would be:

1. Get two slices of bread.
2. Put the filling on one slice.
3. Put the second slice on top.

Programming works in a similar way. Computers need clear instructions about what to do.

Now it's your turn. 🧠

Which instruction is clearer?
